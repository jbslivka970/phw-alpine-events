Program Overview

PHW Colorado Alpine sends informational and customer-care SMS messages for event invitations, reminders, RSVP updates, and member support.

Opt-In and Consent

Members opt in by providing explicit consent in-app or by texting START/YES to +1 (970) 771-0150.
