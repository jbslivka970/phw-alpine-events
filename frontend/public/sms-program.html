SMS Program and Consent

This public page documents the PHW Alpine SMS program, opt-in language, and message templates.

Program Description

Project Healing Waters Colorado Alpine Program sends informational SMS messages to members about event invitations, RSVP reminders, schedule changes, and chapter event logistics.

Program Content Type

Informational and customer-care messaging related to chapter events.

Opt-In Method

Members opt in through a chapter-managed web workflow or member profile update that requires explicit consent before SMS is enabled.

Web opt-in: The user navigates to https://app.phwcoloradoalpine.org and subscribes via the SMS consent form during registration or in Notification Preferences. The form states: "I agree to receive SMS messages from Project Healing Waters Colorado Alpine Program related to event invitations, RSVP reminders, and program updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help." The checkbox is not preselected and consent is not a condition of registration. Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions links are shown below the consent checkbox. Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

Keyword opt-in: Users can text START to +1 (970) 771-0150. Confirmation message: "Project Healing Waters Alpine: You are subscribed to event notifications. Msg frequency varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes."

Members can also opt in by texting START or YES to +1 (970) 771-0150.

Help and Opt-Out

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Message and data rates may apply.

Message Templates

1. Opt-In Confirmation

PHW Alpine: You are subscribed to chapter event texts. Message frequency varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

2. Event Invitation

PHW Alpine: You are invited to Fly Fishing Outing on May 4 at Deckers. RSVP at the event link provided. Reply STOP to opt out.

3. Reminder

PHW Alpine: Reminder: your scheduled event is coming up soon. Check your event link for details. Reply STOP to opt out.

4. Help / Support

PHW Alpine: Help for chapter event texts is available through Colorado Alpine Program leadership. Reply STOP to opt out. See Terms and Privacy pages for details.
