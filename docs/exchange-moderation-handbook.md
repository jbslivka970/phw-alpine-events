# National Gear Exchange Moderation Handbook

Date: 2026-08-31

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| National service owner | Accountable for policy, resources, privacy, and launch/closure decisions |
| National moderator lead | Owns training, queue quality, incident triage, and escalation |
| Exchange moderator | Reviews listings, flags, lifecycle, and routine member issues |
| Exchange administrator | Limited National-approved staff with configuration/user-management authority |
| Program contact | Helps verify local item context; does not automatically receive forum staff access |

PHW tenant roles do not automatically become Flarum moderator or administrator roles. All forum staff access needs National approval, an allowlist, and a periodic review.

## Pilot Approval Workflow

1. A member submits an ISO or Available listing using the required template.
2. A moderator checks category, item relevance, program/city-state, prohibited content, contact safety, and clear shipping/pickup information.
3. Approve, return for revision, reject, or escalate. Apply the correct lifecycle and origin tags.
4. Record only necessary moderation notes. Do not copy personal addresses, private messages, medical information, or tokens into notes.
5. Review `open` listings at the agreed interval. Ask for a status update, mark fulfilled, or close/archive stale topics.

## Flag Triage

| Severity | Examples | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Credible threat, personal safety issue, illegal/regulated goods, account compromise | Hide content, preserve evidence, notify National owner immediately, follow incident process |
| High | Harassment, doxxing, repeat abuse, impersonation, payment fraud attempt | Hide/lock as needed, suspend pending review, escalate within one business day |
| Routine | Off-topic content, incomplete listing, duplicate, stale status | Correct, request revision, merge/close, or educate member |

Moderators must not investigate safety issues alone or promise outcomes beyond PHW policy. In an emergency, use the appropriate emergency-service process before forum administration.

## Enforcement Actions

- Edit only for clarity, safety, or tag correction, and preserve the author’s meaning.
- Hide content that violates policy or exposes personal/sensitive information.
- Lock a discussion when a transfer is complete, dispute is escalated, or replies are unsafe.
- Suspend a user temporarily when needed to contain abuse; National lead reviews longer action.
- Permanently remove access only under the National-approved process and coordinate with PHW membership/identity owners.

## Member Communications

Use calm, factual messages. Explain the relevant rule, the action taken, what can be corrected, and the appeal route. Do not share another member’s information or private moderator discussion.

Suggested decision language:

- Approved: "Your Gear Exchange listing is now visible to PHW members. Please update the status when coordination begins or the item is fulfilled."
- Revision needed: "Please add the item condition, program, city/state, and pickup or shipping details so members can respond safely."
- Not allowed: "This listing cannot be posted because Gear Exchange does not support payments, sales, auctions, or external payment requests."

## Training Checklist

1. Read this handbook and the information architecture.
2. Practice approving, revising, rejecting, hiding, locking, flagging, and suspending with staging data.
3. Complete a privacy and personal-information exercise.
4. Complete a harassment/abuse escalation exercise.
5. Confirm emergency contacts, shift/coverage expectations, and access-removal procedure.
6. Record completion with the moderator lead.

## Quarterly Review

National reviews moderator access, queue age, flags, suspensions, fulfilled rate, stale listings, member feedback, extension risk, and policy gaps at least quarterly. Remove staff access immediately when a person leaves the role.
